The design language should not feel like a modern fintech product with a historical skin. It should feel as though Florin is the digital continuation of the monetary systems of Renaissance Italy—the currency of merchants, republics, guilds, bankers and long-distance trade. The user should feel they are depositing modern stablecoins into an institution descended from the great merchant republics.  ---  # Florin  ## Design Language Memo  ### Renaissance Monetary Infrastructure  ---  # Core Identity  Florin is not merely a stablecoin.
